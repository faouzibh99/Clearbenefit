Why did I get $90 from Social Security?

You probably didn't. The deposit is a one-time Medicare payment for Part B enrollees, paid from the Medicare Improvement Fund. About 20 million people are eligible. Many are not.

Answer four questions to see where you stand, then find out when your money should arrive.

Do you qualify for the $90 payment?

Four questions. Nothing is stored or sent.

1. What kind of Medicare do you have?
2. Does Medicaid or a state program pay your Part B premium?
3. Do you pay an income-related surcharge (IRMAA)?
4. Do you live in the United States?
